## Prompt
Write about the following topic:

Some people think that spending a lot of money on wedding parties and other celebrations is just a waste. Others, however, think that such events are necessary, so spending money on them is acceptable.

Discuss both the views and give your opinion.

Give reasons for your answer and include any relevant examples from your own knowledge or experience.

Write at least 250 words.

## Scoring notes (for the AI evaluator)
- **Task Response requires all three parts:** (1) the view that lavish spending is a waste, (2) the view that such events are necessary and the spending justified, (3) the writer's own clear opinion, stated and maintained throughout. Missing the opinion or covering only one view caps TR at about 5.
- An opinion that sits between the two views ("moderate spending") is fine if it's clearly stated.
- Relevant ideas: debt and financial pressure, social status and showing off, alternative uses of money (savings, education, charity); cultural and family significance, community bonding, the local economy (caterers, venues), once-in-a-lifetime memories.
- Watch for memorised templates ("In this contemporary era…") and off-topic generalisations about all spending.
