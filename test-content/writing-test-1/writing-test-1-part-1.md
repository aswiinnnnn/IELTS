## Prompt
The given bar graph shows the estimated continent-wise increase in food production (in million MT) needed to feed the world population in 2045.

Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

Write at least 150 words.

## Chart data
Title: "Estimated Additional Food Production Needed to Feed World Population in 2045" · unit: million MT · source line: Data from United Nations Food Agriculture Organisation.
Values are read approximately from a 3D chart (±5).

```csv
continent,additional_food_million_mt
Africa,400
Asia,40
North America,20
South America,50
Europe,5
```

## Scoring notes (for the AI evaluator)
- **Expected overview:** Africa needs by far the largest increase (~400m MT, roughly 8× the next region). All other continents need comparatively small amounts, and Europe needs almost none.
- **Key features to compare:** Africa vs the rest; South America (~50) and Asia (~40) are the next highest; North America (~20); Europe minimal (~5).
- Accept figures within ±10 of these values ("about", "approximately"), since the chart is 3D and has no data labels.
- Common errors: describing the data as current production rather than *additional* production needed; giving no overview; listing every bar without comparison.
